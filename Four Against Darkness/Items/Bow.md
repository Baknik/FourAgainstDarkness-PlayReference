A slashing [[Ranged Weapon]] that is held in the offhand, but requires two hands to be fired.

Bows provide no bonus to [[Attack]] rolls.

While outdoors, as an action, [[Ranger]], [[Elf]], and Elf-like classes can make `2` [[Attack]]s with a bow.
