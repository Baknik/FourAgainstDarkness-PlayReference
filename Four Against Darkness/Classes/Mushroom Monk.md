Mushroom Monks are [[Fungal]].
They gain `+L` to any [[Save]] against being restrained.

They do not require [[Ration]]s.
Upon [[Death]], they may be respectfully turned into `d6` [[Ration]]s for the party.

While using a [[Cudgel]], [[Throwing Stars]], or [[Unarmed]], Mushroom Monks can attack up to `Tier` times with a single [[Attack]] action. This is **not** cumulative with other effects that provide additional attacks.

They have the Spores ability with `Tier` uses. As an action, they can use Spores to reduce the `L` of all living [[Minor Enemy|Minor Enemies]] in this [[Tile]] by `1`. [[Fungal]] enemies are immune. This effect can only apply to each enemy once.

Once per [[Long Rest]], as an action, they can use Hyphae to do one of the following:
- Reduce the chance of enemies [[Surprise|Surprising]] the party by `1-in-6` until the end of the [[Adventure]]
- Gain `1` [[Clue]]
- Automatically [[Save]] against an illusion
- Understand the true nature of a shape-shifted creature
